At a lively gathering, you usually…

Think about where you naturally end up when the room is full of people.

  • 1Stay near one trusted person for almost the whole time.
  • 2Talk with a small familiar group in a quiet corner.
  • 3Chat with a few friends, then slip away early.
  • 4Join conversations but take regular breaks on your own.
  • 5Mix socializing with a few quiet moments.
  • 6Move between groups and enjoy the buzz.
  • 7Introduce yourself to new people often.
  • 8Stay in the middle of as many talks as possible.
  • 9Keep the energy going well into the night.
  • 10Thrive as the one hosting or performing.

Pick the option that feels most natural. Every option opens its own page with a short, personalized interpretation before you continue.
