When you first hear a new idea, what pulls your attention the most?

This question looks at how you prefer to take in information.

  • 1The exact steps and facts I can verify right away.
  • 2Clear details, examples, and how things actually work.
  • 3Solid information I can apply to a real task today.
  • 4Practical proofs and what has worked well before.
  • 5A mix of useful facts and the pattern behind them.
  • 6The underlying pattern more than the small details.
  • 7The bigger idea and where it might eventually lead.
  • 8New possibilities and what could be imagined next.
  • 9Symbolic meaning and unexpected creative connections.
  • 10A bold vision of what might exist someday.

Pick the option that feels most natural. Every option opens its own page with a short, personalized interpretation before you continue.
