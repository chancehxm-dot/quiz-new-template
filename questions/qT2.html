When judging whether an idea is good, you focus on…

What do you weigh first when something sounds promising?

  • 1Whether it is logically sound.
  • 2If the reasoning holds up fairly.
  • 3The evidence that supports it.
  • 4How well it survives criticism.
  • 5Both the logic and the people it affects.
  • 6How it lands with the people involved.
  • 7Whether it feels right to those who are hurt.
  • 8The values and care it shows.
  • 9How much it helps the people you love.
  • 10The warmth and support it creates.
