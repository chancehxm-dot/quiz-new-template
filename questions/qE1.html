After a busy, people-filled week, what helps you feel most like yourself again?

This question explores how you naturally recharge. Pick the option that feels closest to you.

  • 1A completely silent evening alone with a book or a movie.
  • 2A calm night at home with just one close person.
  • 3A small, low-key hangout with two or three friends.
  • 4A cozy dinner where the conversation stays slow and easy.
  • 5A casual game night with a handful of familiar people.
  • 6A lively party where you mostly chat with people you know.
  • 7A busy social event where you also meet some new faces.
  • 8A full day of meetings, calls, and group activities.
  • 9A big celebration surrounded by lots of energetic people.
  • 10A packed festival or concert where the crowd fuels you.

Pick the option that feels most natural. Every option opens its own page with a short, personalized interpretation before you continue.
