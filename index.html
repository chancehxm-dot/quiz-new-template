Question 3 of 3

When a friend asks for advice on a hard choice, you usually start with…

This question shows how you weigh decisions. Pick the option that feels most like your natural first move.

Dimension: TF — Thinking-leaning vs Feeling-leaning

  • 1The logical pros and cons laid out as clearly as possible.
  • 2What is fair and consistently reasonable for everyone.
  • 3The most efficient, evidence-based solution.
  • 4The option that holds up best under close scrutiny.
  • 5A balance of the facts and people’s comfort.
  • 6How the decision affects the people involved.
  • 7Keeping harmony and making sure everyone feels heard.
  • 8Protecting people’s feelings and the trust between them.
  • 9What matters most to the people you care about.
  • 10How supported and cared for everyone will end up feeling.
